# Honest status — what is built, designed, and still ahead

Stated plainly, the way the solution intent states it.

## The company brain (Microsoft / Azure)
A working prototype of the brain runs today on a single self-managed server, with memory, skills, governance under human-gated promotion, and per-user access brokering, reached through one connector. The Microsoft and Azure enterprise architecture is a re-platforming of that running prototype, not a greenfield build. It is designed and in evaluation, not yet live. The recommended next step is a scoped pilot on the Microsoft estate against one practice's knowledge.

## The commercial lending brain (AWS)
This is a solution intent prepared for discussion. The underwrite capability, the credit memo agent, is built and running. Early Warning monitoring is the R1 next step. The natural first move is a scoped pilot that routes the credit-memo slice through the brain on the existing estate.

## How to talk about what is ahead
Anything beyond the above is in design or planning rather than live. When something is not yet built, say so plainly and offer the contacts who own the architecture and delivery, rather than implying it already exists.
