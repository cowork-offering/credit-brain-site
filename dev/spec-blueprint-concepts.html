Credit Orrery

Six lifecycle rings orbit one customer core

An orrery reads instantly as a single living system in motion, which is exactly the blueprint's claim: one continuous credit journey, not six disconnected stages. Concentric violet rings make the inside-out architecture legible at a glance while sub-activity nodes give the close-up density an award jury rewards.

AnimatesAll six stage-rings rotate slowly at different speeds (inner fastest, outer slowest), each at its own tilt so the whole orrery reads as a 3D hologram. Scroll advances a bright comet of light around the rings in request order Prospecting to Servicing, igniting each activity node as it passes, then the outermost Servicing ring closes the loop with a feedback arc back to the core to show the always-on monitoring cycle.
5-layer tieThe radius IS the 5-layer stack read inside-out: the pulsing core is 01 Brain and Agent Orchestration (the RM/customer decision center); the inner halo is 02 Model Substrate (scoring, spreading, rating intelligence); the mid band of MCP-style connector ticks is 03 Tools and MCP Servers (CRM, Onboarding, Loan Origination, Core Banking platforms); the data-ring lattice is 04 Data Foundation (Single Customer View, Insights, MI); and the outer engagement arc carrying the six rotating stage-rings is 05 Systems of Record and Engagement (Web/App, RMs, portals, chatbots). The six lifecycle stages sweep across all five layers.

The three parallel lines (Sales, Risk, Servicing) make explicit that commercial credit is not one track but braided workstreams that hand off at interchanges, which is exactly the "streamline and automate the handoffs" thesis of the blueprint.

AnimatesThe whole metro map is a translucent 3D slab that slowly yaws on its vertical axis (about 12 degrees each way), so stations parallax and the glow tubes catch additive light as they rotate. Scroll drives a bright deal-train pulse that travels left to right along the lines: each scroll section lights the next interchange (Prospecting through Servicing), draws in that stage's stations, and at the bottom a 4-layer architecture rail slides up under the active stage to show which platform/engagement systems power it.
5-layer tieThe four blueprint architecture tiers running beneath the tracks map to methodology 05 Systems of Record (Core Banking / Client Ecosystems baseboard), 04 Data Foundation (Loan Origination + Single Customer View), 03 Tools & MCP Servers (Customer Insights, Next Best Actions, Customisable MI as the analytics interchange logic), and 02 Model Substrate + 01 Brain & Agent Orchestration fused in the Virtual RM / Data & AI signal box that dispatches the train and switches the tracks at every interchange.

It stays in the case study's violet-hologram DNA family but is its own organism: where the helix coils and the plate stack layers flatly, the Arbor grows upward and outward, giving the functional customer journey a living, dimensional silhouette that photographs beautifully on near-black.

Pass-branches brighten to #c29bf5 and breach/decline-branches dim to a faint stub, so the canopy's final shape encodes the journey's outcomes.
